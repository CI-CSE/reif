# Reif is Research Eiffel
Welcome to the Reif website! Reif is a research version of
[EiffelStudio](https://eiffel.com). Reif integrates
research projects such as AutoProof, AutoFix and others
among each other and with EiffelStudio itself.

Reif is being developed at the [Chair of Software Engineering](https://se.constructor.ch/)
of [Constructor Institute of Technology](https://institute.constructor.org/).

Reif is a fork of
[EVE](https://se.inf.ethz.ch/research/eve/) which was developed
at the [Chair of Software Engineering](https://se.inf.ethz.ch/)
at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/).


## AutoProof
[AutoProof]({% link autoproof/index.md %}) is an auto-active verifier
for functional properties of the Eiffel programming language.
It uses the [Boogie verifier](https://github.com/boogie-org/boogie) as a back-end.

To get started with AutoProof take a look at the
[AutoProof quick start guide]({% link autoproof/quick-start.md %}).

A more systematic description of AutoProof is in the
[AutoProof tutorial]({% link _tutorial/index.md %}).
